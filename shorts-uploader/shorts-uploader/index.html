IKDE Shorts Uploader

IKDE Shorts Uploader is a private, self-hosted tool. The operator uses it to upload and schedule short videos he has edited to YouTube channels he is authorized to manage.

Use of the YouTube Data API

  • Uploading videos to the connected channel, as private videos with a scheduled publish time.
  • Reading the connected channel's ID and the status of videos uploaded by this tool, to confirm that an upload reached the right channel.

Every upload is reviewed and approved manually by the operator before it happens. The tool does not collect data about viewers or other YouTube users. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

IKDE Shorts Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
