Privacy Policy

This policy explains how IKDE Shorts Uploader ("the app") handles Google user data when a YouTube channel is connected.

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

1. Data the app accesses

An OAuth token for the YouTube permissions you approve. With it the app uploads videos to your channel and reads your channel ID and the status of videos it uploaded. The app does not access your email, contacts, comments, subscribers, analytics or data about other YouTube users.

2. How the data is used

Only to upload videos that the operator has manually approved and to check that they reached the correct channel with the correct schedule. Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling or training AI models.

3. Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer, in a file readable only by the operator. No cloud database or third-party server is used.

4. Cookies and device data

The app and this website do not set cookies and do not store, access or collect information on your device or browser.

5. Sharing

Google user data is not sold, transferred or shared with third parties. Videos are sent only to YouTube.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked or deletion is requested. You can revoke the app's access to your data at any time via the Google security settings page at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. On request by email, all stored tokens and data related to your channel are deleted within 7 days.
